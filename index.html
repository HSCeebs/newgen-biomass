What is Eco-Leaf Board?

Bridging the Gap Between Agriculture and Construction

Eco-Leaf is a proprietary, high-density bio-composite panel engineered entirely from upcycled oil palm fronds and an advanced epoxy resin system. Developed as a direct response to the massive agricultural waste generated by the palm oil industry, it transforms discarded biomass into a high-value structural asset.

Designed to replace traditional plywood, MDF, and even porcelain tiles, Eco-Leaf boards are completely hydrophobic (waterproof), emit zero toxic VOCs like formaldehyde, and boast structural strength comparable to natural timber without the grain-direction weaknesses. It is a true circular economy product built for modern, sustainable construction.

The Innovation

Solving the Problems: The Eco-Leaf Advantage

Eco-Leaf utilizes proprietary "High-Density Compression" to create a structural composite that fully valorizes the natural strength of the palm frond.


The Manufacturing Process

1. PREPARATION

Shredding leaves (e.g. Oil Palm Leaves) to create uniform fiber size.

2. MIXING

Blending fiber with epoxy resin & hardener for uniform coating.

3. COMPRESSION

Hot Pressing in custom mold to densify the material (optimized >60% fiber ratio).

4. CURING

24 Hour Controlled Curing for final structural strength.

Economics & Future Value

Bridging the Gap Between Wood and Tiles

  • Raw Material (Fronds): Near-Zero Cost. As an agricultural waste residue, the primary cost is logistics, providing a massive buffer against fluctuating timber prices.
  • Binder (Epoxy System): The primary cost driver. Modeled conservatively on industrial bulk purchasing at a 2:1 fiber-rich compression ratio to optimize efficiency.
  • Processing: Estimated energy costs for industrial shredding, drying, and hydraulic pressing.
