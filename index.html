The Vision

Transforming Agricultural Waste into Sarawak's Green Gold

A Strategic Initiative to Transform Agricultural Biomass into High-Value Assets, Supporting Sarawak's PCDS 2030 Goals for a Green Economy[cite: 2].

Our Mission

We aim to transform a massive agricultural liability, oil palm frond waste, into a high-value, globally competitive material for the furniture and construction industries[cite: 11].

PCDS 2030 Alignment

  • Environmental Sustainability: Implements a Circular Economy model by capturing oil palm fronds and transitioning away from the "linear" waste model[cite: 36, 37, 38].
  • Economic Prosperity: Shifts the economic value from raw materials to proprietary, IP-protected innovation (UI2025006487)[cite: 46, 47].
  • Social Inclusivity: Creates high-value jobs for TVET and university graduates (e.g., machine operators, R&D staff)[cite: 58, 59, 60].

The Innovation

Beyond Plywood: The Ecoleaf Advantage

Ecoleaf is a proprietary, patent-pending (UI2025006487) composite panel engineered from oil palm fronds and a high-performance epoxy resin system[cite: 13].

Key Features

  • Waterproof & Durable: Solves the swelling and delamination issues of standard plywood[cite: 15]. Ecoleaf's epoxy encapsulation creates a hydrophobic barrier around every fiber chip[cite: 132].
  • Zero-VOC: Eliminates toxic formaldehyde emissions found in standard furniture boards[cite: 16].
  • Sustainable: Upcycles 100% agricultural waste and reduces open burning[cite: 17].

The Manufacturing Process

  1. Precision Preparation: Raw fronds are shredded and industrially dried to a moisture content of <10%[cite: 119, 120].
  2. Homogenous Amalgamation: Dried fibers are mixed with the epoxy system using high-torque industrial mixers[cite: 122].
  3. High-Pressure Densification: The mixture is subjected to high-pressure hydraulic pressing to eliminate voids and cure the material[cite: 126, 128, 129].

The Sarawak Challenge

Solving the RM 800 Million Disconnect

The Disconnect

While Crude Palm Oil contributed RM 801 million to state coffers in 2025, Timber Products contributed only RM 21 million[cite: 70, 71]. We export finite resources and import finished goods[cite: 67]. Ecoleaf bridges this gap[cite: 73].

The Open Burning Crisis

Millions of tonnes of old palm oil fronds are pruned annually[cite: 78]. Farmers frequently resort to open burning to clear this waste, exacerbating local and regional haze[cite: 81, 83].

The Quality Gap

Standard engineered woods (plywood, MDF) are inherently hygroscopic[cite: 95]. In Borneo's hyper-humid climate, this leads to irreversible thickness swelling and rapid delamination[cite: 98].
