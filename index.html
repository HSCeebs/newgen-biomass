The Innovation

Beyond Plywood: The Ecoleaf Advantage

Ecoleaf is a proprietary, patent-pending (UI2025006487) composite panel engineered from oil palm fronds and a high-performance epoxy resin system.

Waterproof & Durable

Solves the swelling and delamination issues of standard plywood. Ecoleaf's epoxy encapsulation creates a hydrophobic barrier around every fiber chip.

Zero-VOC Safety

Eliminates toxic formaldehyde emissions found in standard furniture boards, ensuring healthier indoor environments.

100% Sustainable

Upcycles pure agricultural waste, permanently sequestering carbon and directly reducing open burning practices.


The Manufacturing Process

  1. Precision Preparation: Raw fronds are shredded and industrially dried to a moisture content of <10%.
  2. Homogenous Amalgamation: Dried fibers are mixed with the epoxy system using high-torque industrial mixers.
  3. High-Pressure Densification: The mixture is subjected to high-pressure hydraulic pressing to eliminate voids and cure the material.

The Malaysia Challenge

Solving the RM 800 Million Disconnect

1. The Economic Disconnect

While Crude Palm Oil contributed RM 801 million to state coffers in 2025, Timber Products contributed only RM 21 million. We export finite resources and import finished goods. Ecoleaf bridges this gap.

2. The Open Burning Crisis

Millions of tonnes of old palm oil fronds are pruned annually. Farmers frequently resort to open burning to clear this waste, exacerbating local and regional haze.

3. The Quality Gap

Standard engineered woods (plywood, MDF) are inherently hygroscopic. In Borneo's hyper-humid climate, this leads to irreversible thickness swelling and rapid delamination.
