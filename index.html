What is Leafit?

Bridging the Gap Between Agriculture and Construction

Leafit is a proprietary, high-density bio-composite panel engineered entirely from upcycled oil palm fronds and an advanced epoxy resin system. Developed as a direct response to the massive agricultural waste generated by the palm oil industry, it transforms discarded biomass into a high-value structural asset.

Designed to replace traditional plywood, MDF, and even porcelain tiles, Leafit is highly water resistant, emits zero toxic VOCs like formaldehyde, and boasts structural strength comparable to natural timber without the grain-direction weaknesses. It is a true circular economy product built for modern, sustainable construction.

At a glance

  • Made from upcycled oil palm fronds
  • High-density compression with epoxy resin
  • Highly water resistant & rot resistant
  • Zero toxic VOC emissions
  • Timber-like strength, no grain-direction weakness

The Innovation

Solving the Problems: The Leafit Advantage

Leafit utilizes proprietary “High-Density Compression” to create a structural composite that fully valorizes the natural strength of the palm frond.

The Manufacturing Process

1
PREPARATION

Shredding leaves (e.g. Oil Palm Leaves) to create uniform fiber size.

2
MIXING

Blending fiber with epoxy resin & hardener for uniform coating.

3
COMPRESSION

Hot pressing in custom mold to densify the material (optimized >60% fiber ratio).

4
CURING

24-hour controlled curing for final structural strength.
